Hello, I'm Joshua Pope. I'm a gameplay designer and solo developer!

About Joshua Pope

Joshua Pope is a junior Games and Interactive Media major at Michigan State University. Joshua has worked on many games while at the university including (but not limited to) several prototypes, game jams works, and solo projects.

While not developing games, Joshua participates as a performer in many scholastic and professional ensembles. Some of the high level ensembles he has played in include MSU's own Spartan Marching Band, Spartan Brass, Phantom Regiment Drum & Bugle Corps, and Nashville Music Scoring.
