Intro

Hello, I'm Joshua Pope. I'm a gameplay designer and solo developer!

About Joshua Pope

Joshua Pope is a junior Games and Interactive Media major at Michigan State University. Joshua has worked on many games while at the university including (but not limited to) several prototypes, game jams works, and solo projects.

While not developing games, Joshua participates as a performer in many scholastic and professional ensembles. Some of the high level ensembles he has played in include MSU's own Spartan Marching Band, Spartan Brass, Phantom Regiment Drum & Bugle Corps, and Nashville Music Scoring.

Project Showcase

Here are some of the projects I've worked on:

Text-Based RPG
A print & play RPG where the player takes on the guise of an adventurer. Take on the world one card at a time. If you feel up to it, explore the depths of a ruthless and terrifying dungeon, where the rewards may be well worth the risks.
Nozama
A 2D tapper clone. This was a group project created during a game jam. Take on the role of an overworked Nozama employee and defy odds as you sort boxes in this addicting arcade game.
Narwhal Obby
An "obby" is the Roblox technical term for obstacle course. This one just happened to be created by a Narwhal, and was designed to be incredibly difficult.
War of Attira
A print & play draft of a brand new strategy oriented board game. This game takes inspiration from games like chess and checkers and merges them with themes from RTS games.
The Game Jammed
An exciting action adventure game in which the player must find there way through hordes of corrupted robots after crash landing in a strange new land. The player, however, is also dealing with their own malfunctions.
